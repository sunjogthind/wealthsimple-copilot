# Demo Video Script — Wealthsimple Copilot

**Target length: 2:30–3:00**
**Tone: Confident, clear, no filler. Show, don't tell.**

---

## [0:00–0:20] The Hook

*Show the Wealthsimple app (or a screenshot of it).*

"Wealthsimple gives 3 million Canadians the tools to invest. But it doesn't tell you if you're investing *well*. Your trading app doesn't talk to your tax software. Your behavioral blind spots stay invisible. And if you want to consolidate your finances onto the platform, you're on your own.

This is Wealthsimple Copilot."

*Cut to the Copilot dashboard.*

---

## [0:20–0:40] Architecture Overview (Brief)

*Show the module cards on the dashboard: Trading Coach, Tax Optimizer, Migration Planner (coming soon), Smart Support (coming soon).*

"Copilot is a modular AI agent that lives inside the app. It has a single conversational interface backed by specialized modules. Today I'll show you two that are fully working: the Trading Coach and the Tax Optimizer."

---

## [0:40–1:40] Trading Coach Demo (Core)

*Show CSV upload.*

"I'm uploading 10 months of Wealthsimple trade history. The Copilot analyzes every trade."

*Show the streaming response with bias cards appearing.*

"It immediately identifies that I have a strong disposition effect — I sold NVDA at a 5% gain after 4 days, but held AC.TO through a 19% decline for 8 months. It also flags that 60% of my portfolio is concentrated in Canadian bank stocks."

*Show the specific bias cards with evidence.*

"Every insight is backed by specific trades from my own data. This isn't generic advice — it's a mirror."

*Type a pre-trade check: "I'm thinking of buying 50 more shares of SHOP"*

"Now I ask: should I buy more SHOP? The Copilot checks my history."

*Show the yellow/red flag response.*

"It flags that I've bought and sold SHOP three times in the last 10 months, each time buying back at a worse price. It's asking me — what's different this time? The decision is mine. The awareness is the Copilot's."

---

## [1:40–2:10] Tax Optimizer Demo

*Click on Tax Optimizer module or type "do I have any tax harvesting opportunities?"*

"Switching to the Tax Optimizer. It scans my non-registered holdings for unrealized losses."

*Show harvesting candidates with estimated savings.*

"It finds two candidates and estimates $700 in potential tax savings. But look — it flags that SHOP.TO has a superficial loss risk because I've been buying and selling it repeatedly. It tells me the exact date I'd need to wait until to safely harvest."

"And the critical thing: it tells me to verify with my accountant before acting. The AI identifies the opportunity. The human makes the call."

---

## [2:10–2:40] Extensibility + Human Boundary

*Show the sidebar with all 4 modules visible.*

"The architecture is modular. The same router and conversational interface extends to future modules — a migration planner that helps users consolidate assets onto Wealthsimple, and context-aware support that goes beyond what Willow does today. Each module is a new skill the agent learns."

*Brief pause.*

"The design principle is simple: the Copilot expands your ability to make good financial decisions. It never makes them for you. Every action that moves money or triggers a tax event stays in your hands. That's not a limitation — it's the whole point."

---

## [2:40–2:50] Close

"This is what Wealthsimple's AI layer should look like — not a chatbot answering FAQs, but an intelligent copilot that sees your complete financial picture and helps you act on it."

*Show the URL.*

"Wealthsimple Copilot. Try it live at [URL]."

---

## Production Notes

- Screen record the actual working app — no slides, no mockups
- Use the mock CSV data from config/mock-trades.csv (designed to trigger interesting biases)
- Keep mouse movements deliberate and slow enough for viewers to follow
- Use a clean browser window (no bookmarks bar, no other tabs visible)
- Record audio separately if possible for better quality
- If you stumble, just re-record that section — don't try to do it in one take
- Export as MP4, 1080p minimum
